Privacy & Data Mandate

Establishing the operational standards for how Spendly processes and safeguards your financial intelligence.

Version 2026.04 • Last Audit: March 2026

{{ icon('data-usage', 'text-[18px]') }}

1. Data Ingestion

We only process data that you explicitly authorize and input into the terminal. This includes account credentials for secure access and transactional metadata (amounts, categories, memos) for ledger maintenance.

{{ icon('psychology', 'text-[18px]') }}

2. Intelligence Processing

Your data is processed locally within our secure application environment to generate visual insights, budget threshold monitoring, and fiscal forecasting. We do not use your financial history to train external machine learning models.

{{ icon('shield', 'text-[18px]') }}

3. Security Infrastructure

All ledger entries are stored in encrypted database clusters. While no digital vault is impenetrable, we implement industry-standard cryptographic safeguards and recommend all operators utilize complex, unique passphrases.

{{ icon('handshake', 'text-[18px]') }}

4. Third-Party Policy

Spendly maintains a strictly zero-sale policy. We never lease or sell your capital history to advertisers or brokers. External data flow is restricted to essential infrastructure providers (hosting, email SMTP) required to maintain uptime.

For comprehensive inquiries regarding our data protocols, reach out to our legal console: support@spendly.app
